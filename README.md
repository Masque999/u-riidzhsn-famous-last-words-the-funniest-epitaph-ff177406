# Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

One original line, at most 150 characters: the funniest possible famous last words as a single tombstone epitaph.

Entry: `Do not stand at my grave and weep. I am not there; I am in the cloud, and the subscription renews forever.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
